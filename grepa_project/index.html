Introduction

GREPA SERVICES (Pty) Ltd is a licensed service private company based in Westonaria and Welkom, South Africa. The company will be based in every corner of South Africa and it was established in 2019. We currently operate in Cleaning, Pipes & Steel Work Projects, Shutdowns, Repairs, Maintenance, Demolishing, Bush & Grass Cutting, Construction, Labour Hire, Mining and Engineering.

We have been able to secure all the required clearances and office facilities to run a private company and we are aware that to run a standard multi or variant services company can be demanding which is why we are equipped to supply or perform excellently well.

Our company is a client-focused and result driven multi services company that provides broad-based services and we will work hard to meet and surpass our clients' expectations whenever they contact their service concerns to our organisation. At GREPA SERVICES, our client's best interest would always come first and everything we do is guided by our values and ethics.

Our plan is to position our company to become one of the leading brands in the multi services industry and this might look like a tall dream but we are optimistic that this will surely come to pass because we have done our research and feasibility studies.
