WHO WE ARE

A capable partner for demanding work.

GREPA SERVICES (Pty) Ltd is a South African multi-service company based in Westonaria and Welkom. We provide responsive, quality-led services across cleaning, construction, engineering, maintenance and labour support.

We bring a client-first mindset to every brief. Our team focuses on understanding the job, working safely, and delivering work that meets the required standard.
